# Understanding Photosynthesis: Energy Conversion in Plants

Photosynthesis is the fundamental biological process by which autotrophic organisms convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.

## Inputs of Photosynthesis
Plants take in water (H2O) through roots and carbon dioxide (CO2) through leaf stomata. Sunlight provides light energy.

## Chlorophyll Function
Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, the primary green pigment. Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light while reflecting green light. Photons excite electrons in chlorophyll, driving photolysis of water.
