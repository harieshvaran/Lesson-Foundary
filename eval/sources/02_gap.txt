# Overview of Plant Energy Production

Plants produce their own food through a process called photosynthesis. This process combines raw materials from the environment to manufacture organic compounds.

## Raw Materials and Products

Plants gather water from the soil through their roots and carbon dioxide from the surrounding air through pores in their leaves. When these materials are combined under appropriate environmental conditions, the plant manufactures glucose.

Glucose is used by the plant for energy, growth, and cellular repair. Excess glucose is converted into starch for long-term storage in roots and seeds.

During this conversion process, plants produce oxygen gas as a byproduct, which exits the leaves and enters the surrounding atmosphere.

In summary:
- Inputs: Water and Carbon Dioxide
- Outputs: Glucose and Oxygen
